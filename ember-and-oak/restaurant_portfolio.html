Our
Story

The Beginning

Born from fire,
built on trust

Ember & Oak opened in 2019 in a converted Victorian warehouse in the heart of the city. What began as a stubborn belief that wood fire could unlock flavours no gas stove ever would quickly became something more — a gathering place, a ritual, a second home for those who love eating well.

Our dining room seats just 48. That's intentional. We believe great hospitality requires attention — to the plate, to the guest, to the moment. We'll never grow faster than our ability to care.

We buy from seven family farms within 100 miles of this kitchen. We don't print menus until the morning of service, because the farm decides what we cook — not the other way around.

The Team

Meet the people
behind the fire

NF
Nadia Ferrara
Head Chef & Co-Founder

Trained under Bittor Arginzoniz in the Basque Country, Nadia returned home to pioneer live-fire cooking in this city. Three-time award winner, fearless technician, and tireless advocate for local farmers.

MS
Marco Sela
Sommelier & Co-Founder

Marco spent a decade working in Burgundy, Andalusia, and the Douro Valley before co-founding Ember & Oak. His 200-label list champions natural producers who share our respect for the land.

AL
Ada Liu
Pastry Chef

Ada brings precision and whimsy to the dessert menu in equal measure. She believes a perfect meal deserves an equally perfect ending — and she's usually right.

What We Believe

Our values

01
Fire is a craft

Every cook at Ember & Oak spends their first month learning the hearth — how it breathes, how it varies hour to hour, how to read its heat. Fire is not a shortcut. It's an instrument.

02
The farm comes first

We build relationships with producers before we build menus. If a farm is having a bad season, we feature it, support it, buy more of it. Their success is our story.

03
Hospitality is human

No scripts, no upselling, no formula. We train our team to read a table — when to be present, when to disappear, when a joke is right.

04
Small is intentional

48 seats is our ceiling and our pride. We will never compromise the experience for the sake of scale. Quality is geometry here: the smaller the space, the closer the attention.
